FICTIONAL DEMONSTRATION POLICY - FOR ASSIGNMENT & TESTING PURPOSES ONLY
Organization: NovaCorp Demo Enterprises
Document: Expense Reimbursement and IT Support Policy (v1.0)

Section 1: Expense Reimbursement Process
Employees may claim reimbursement for approved business travel, client meals within daily caps, and pre-approved training or home-office accessories.
Claims must be submitted within 30 calendar days of the expense date with itemized receipts attached.
Finance reviews and disburses approved reimbursements within 10 business days.

Section 2: IT Security Policy
Employees must use strong unique passwords, enable Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) on all company accounts, and lock screens when stepping away.
Connecting to public unsecured Wi-Fi for sensitive work requires the NovaCorp VPN.
Sharing credentials, bypassing endpoint controls, or installing unverified software is strictly prohibited.

Section 3: Hardware and Software Support
Standard laptops and peripherals are issued at onboarding and maintained by IT Infrastructure.
For hardware faults, VPN issues, or software license requests, employees should raise an IT support ticket with the issue category, priority, and description.
High-priority incidents affecting work continuity are targeted for initial response within 4 business hours.
