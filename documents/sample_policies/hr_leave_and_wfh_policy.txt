FICTIONAL DEMONSTRATION POLICY - FOR ASSIGNMENT & TESTING PURPOSES ONLY
Organization: NovaCorp Demo Enterprises
Document: HR Leave and Work-From-Home Policy (v1.0)

Section 1: Annual Leave Policy
Full-time employees accrue annual leave based on tenure, up to 14-18 days per calendar year depending on band.
Annual leave requests for 3 or more consecutive days require at least 5 business days of advance notice and manager approval.
Up to 5 unused annual leave days may carry over into Q1 of the following calendar year; remaining unused days lapse on March 31.

Section 2: Sick Leave Policy
Employees are entitled to 8 to 10 paid sick leave days per calendar year for personal illness, injury, or medical appointments.
A medical certificate is required if sick leave exceeds 3 consecutive working days.

Section 3: Maternity Leave Policy
Eligible female employees receive up to 26 weeks of paid maternity leave in accordance with statutory guidelines.
Requests should be submitted to HR at least 30 days prior to the expected start date, accompanied by medical documentation.

Section 4: Work-From-Home (WFH) Policy
Eligible hybrid employees may work from home up to 2 days per week, subject to team coverage and prior manager approval.
Working from home does not imply automatic approval for any specific date (such as a future Friday); employees must submit a WFH request in the HR portal and receive manager sign-off.
Employees working remotely must remain reachable during core hours (10:00 AM - 4:00 PM local time) on an approved network.
